# US B2B cold-calling hours: planning guidance

Saved November 23, 2026. General information, not legal advice or a completed 50-state compliance review. Recheck current law before launching or changing campaigns.

## Federal distinction

For genuine business-to-business sales calls placed by a live human to a business line, there is no single nationwide federal earliest-start time. The FCC's familiar 8 a.m.–9 p.m. local-time solicitation restriction expressly addresses residential telephone subscribers; it is not blanket permission or a blanket restriction for every B2B call.

Ordinary B2B solicitations are exempt from many FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule provisions, but the exemption is not total. In particular, the FTC's 2024 amendments extended prohibitions against material misrepresentations and false or misleading statements to B2B telemarketing. State laws, number type, calling method, registration requirements, and other applicable restrictions still require review.

## Application to this campaign

- Live human, manually placed call to a verified business landline: generally outside the federal residential calling-hours restriction; review applicable state requirements.
- An owner's cellphone, residential line, or shared home/business number: do not assume a B2B listing establishes exemption from residential/wireless protections.
- AI/artificial-voice, prerecorded calls, and certain automated dialing methods: separate TCPA consent requirements may apply, especially to wireless numbers. B2B purpose alone does not exempt these calls. A manual-call task queue does not authorize outbound AI calls.
- Immediately suppress do-not-call requests. Identify the caller truthfully; do not use misleading claims or caller ID.

## Proposed company policy, not activated

Use weekday calling windows of 9 a.m.–5 p.m. at the prospect's actual location, beginning no earlier than the business's opening time, subject to state-specific legal review and applicable restrictions. This is a conservative proposed policy, not a legal safe harbor or evidence of statistically optimum hours.

An 8 a.m. start may be permissible in some circumstances, but do not authorize nationwide 8 a.m. or earlier calling across mixed directory lists without checking state requirements and classifying numbers. A 24-hour emergency-service listing is not permission for overnight sales solicitations.

One representative owns each niche across timezone queues. Discord is intended to announce timezone transitions based on approved calling windows. Use actual prospect location and daylight-saving-aware IANA timezones; an area code alone is unreliable for mobile numbers. Do not infer permission for @everyone mentions. Saving these notes does not implement notifications or change workflow schedules.
