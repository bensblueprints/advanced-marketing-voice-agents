### Review before representative use

- The PDF advertises a seven-day free trial and month-to-month/no-contract terms. These are not confirmed offer terms. The previously approved introductory price was $100/week; obtain owner approval before offering different terms.
- Twenty simultaneous calls, immediate SMS alerts, ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro/Jobber integrations, and instant human/emergency transfers must not be promised unless configured and verified for that customer.
- Business practice demos do not have connected human transfers. The sales host's separately configured owner transfer is not proof that a customer's on-call technician is connected.
- Do not claim that a personalized agent has already been built or connected unless its route is verified. Distinguish a generic demonstration from an actual contractor's services and pricing.
- Practice appointments are fictional. Real appointments require verified availability, date/timezone clarification, real identity, explicit consent, and successful booking confirmation. Do not automatically book someone simply because they tried the demo.
- Honor do-not-call requests immediately and independently of whether a prospect agrees to test the demo. Send follow-up texts only under an approved, compliant consent process.
- Gas leaks and other life-safety emergencies require an approved emergency-safety protocol. A transfer attempt is not emergency response and must not replace appropriate evacuation/emergency-service instructions.
